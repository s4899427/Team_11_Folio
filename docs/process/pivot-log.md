# Pivot Log

Dated record of major direction changes, for the Design Process Overview.

## Pivot 1 — Wiki for all world culture → Music × Art × Fashion
**Why**: Original scope (a wiki covering all of world culture — music,
fashion, art, food) was too broad to build a genuine proof-of-concept
against. Narrowed to three verticals to make depth possible.

## Pivot 2 — Three separate verticals → Unified Almanac pattern
**Why**: Music initially had its own streaming/search/playlist UI, separate
from Art and Fashion's browse-by-era model. Unified all three into one
consistent pattern (browse by era/location/style) for coherence and to
remove the need for real music licensing infrastructure.

## Pivot 3 — Full music streaming → Short preview clips + Spotify link-out
**Why**: Real full-catalog streaming isn't legally buildable by a student
project. Kept music present as context (artist bios, 30-second previews)
rather than cutting it entirely.

## Pivot 4 — Single "accuracy" model → Plurality of perspectives
**Why**: In response to peer feedback (Team 8) asking who decides accuracy
when sources disagree. Realised cultural/interpretive content isn't
fact-checkable the way a citation is — art interpretation is subjective,
culture evolves, fashion has no single correct read. Multiple attributed
perspectives shown side by side, rather than merged into one enforced
version. Verifiable facts (dates, locations) remain a separate, smaller
dispute process.

## Pivot 5 — Browse-only app → Today's Thread + Archive + Me
**Why**: Course feedback (professor) identified the daily curated
"experience" concept as the strongest direction. Restructured the whole
app around one daily Thread (bounded, locks at midnight) as the front door,
with the original browsing experience becoming the Archive.

## Pivot 6 — Fixed 3-part Thread → Any number of elements, any domain
**Why**: Professor feedback specifically pushed back on locking a Thread to
exactly one song + one artwork + one outfit — "it should be an experience."
Thread model generalised to hold any number of elements from any cultural
domain (added a "Place" element as proof).

## Pivot 7 — No coordination feature → "Tonight's Theme"
**Why**: Several interviews (Marushka, Sully, Rayyan) independently
described checking friends' style/music before an occasion. Built a
lightweight, non-social-graph feature demonstrating this directly, rather
than a full follow/messaging system.

## Pivot 8 — Name change: Motif → Folio
**Why**: "Motif" was found to collide heavily with existing products
(Yamaha's synthesizer line, multiple apps). Renamed to avoid confusion with
existing products.
