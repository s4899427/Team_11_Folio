# Thursday Talking Points — Finalize Topic + Build Slides

## 1. Open with the vibe (2 min)
- "Social + mobile app where music and fashion are one shared culture, not two apps"
- No reels/videos — interactive, reading + listening + visual, not video-heavy
- Not solving a problem — it's about fun, discovery, self-expression
- Not just hip-hop — any genre, any culture, any language

## 2. Bring in the core mechanic — "Drop & Remix" (3 min)
- Explain simply: "Users post a track + an outfit together — that's a 'Drop.' Other people can remix it — swap the song or the fit, keep the other half, and repost their version."
- Why it matters: this is the actual *social/collaborative* part the course wants to see (not just a feed)
- Frame as: "This is the fun, shareable bit — like a chain reaction of style/sound"

## 3. Mention AR — light touch (1-2 min)
- "One feature we want to try: pick a top + bottom and see it composed on a figure — like a simple dress-up tool"
- Keep it framed as *one feature to explore*, not a hard requirement — scope depends on time/skills
- Don't get into markerless vs. marker-based tracking here — that's a build-phase conversation

## 4. Mention nearby events — light touch (1 min)
- "We also want a tab showing festivals/gigs/art events happening nearby — ties into the whole 'what's culturally happening right now' idea"

## 5. Lock the domain + name (2 min)
- Confirm: Social & Mobile Computing — cultural expression, location-aware, collaborative
- Decide on app name if not already locked (shortlist: Motif, Static, or team's pick)

## 6. Divide and build the 5 slides in class
- Slide 1: Team & strengths
- Slide 2: Domain/problem space
- Slide 3: Design opportunity + concepts (Drop & Remix, critique of Spotify/Zara/AR try-on/Upstream)
- Slide 4: Audience (festival-goer vs. commuter personas + specific sub-groups)
- Slide 5: Plan of work (research phase: journals → interviews → synthesize → AR decision → demo content)

## 7. Confirm next steps before leaving
