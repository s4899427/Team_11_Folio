# Folio artist bench and used-artist archive

Why this file exists: Folio's Edition is picked by humans, and the app does **not** check whether an artist has appeared before (checked in `prototype/src`: no repeat check). So "no repeats" is a **curator rule, kept by hand here**. Before publishing any Edition, check this file. After publishing, add the pick to the Used table.

## Rule
- **Content is never repeated: the same track or release appears in at most one Edition.** An artist may return with a new track or release. Check the Used table first, then add.
- A returning artist counts as established, not new, so they do not count toward the 2 to 4 new-artist mix (team decision; change it here if you decide otherwise).
- New-artist mix per Edition: at least 2, at most 4, never over 45% (Studio rule).
- A bench name is NOT cleared until a curator opens the artist's own page, finds an independent source on a different site, listens, and ticks the attestation. Names below come from AI brainstorming (Google AI Mode) and were only partly verified. See `AI_USE.md`.
