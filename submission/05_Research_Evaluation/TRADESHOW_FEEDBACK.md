# Tradeshow peer feedback on Folio (summary)

**Source.** The course's "DECO3500 Tradeshow Feedback" Miro board, where students from other teams answered set questions about each team they saw. Seven written responses about Folio were readable to us, from peers in four other teams (teams 6, 7, 8 and 10). The responses are paraphrased and de-identified here: no names or student numbers are kept in this repository. The board itself is the primary record. We did not choose the respondents, so this is not a sample, and we did not follow anyone up. Some responses were only partly visible to us; we may have missed others.

**How to read it.** This is formative peer feedback after a short demonstration. It is not user research with real listeners, curators or artists. We have **not** retested or acted on most of it. Where we later built something related, that is said below, with no claim that the feedback caused it.

## What peers said, by theme

| # | Theme | What was said (paraphrased) | Status in Folio |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The social flow is mostly one-way** | Four peers (T6, T7, T7, T8) said interaction is thin: listeners read what a curator picked but cannot easily sense others reading, reply, or add songs; it can feel like a music information page more than a social app. Suggestions: make participation visible without turning it into a popularity contest (moderated reflections, local discussion, collaborative Editions); make artist Connect two-way; link gigs to venue or ticket pages. | Comments existed. A "reading now" count was added on 24 Oct, after the tradeshow; it may partly address "sensing others", which we have not checked with these peers. Two-way artist Connect, moderation and venue links are not built. |
| 2 | **Real versus demo-only was unclear** | Three peers (T7, T8, T10) said it was hard to tell which features really work and which are simulated (for example the team acting as founding curators, a demo artist for Connect). Suggested labelling demo features and a clearer user flow, plus a clickable prototype for the curation flow. | Not done on the live site. Sandbox mode shows a demo banner, but the live app does not mark what is simulated. |
| 3 | **The midnight lock is hard to see and can cost a reader a link** | Two peers (T8) said a short demo cannot show the midnight lock (a "jump to midnight" switch was suggested), and that a reader who finds an artist near midnight may lose the artist's details or event link. | Not built. Past Editions stay readable in the archive, and the archive is now a month calendar (added after the tradeshow). |
| 4 | **Research should be tied to design more visibly** | Two peers (T8, T10) said the poster showed the process (concept, interviews, Week 9 feedback, music pivot, Folio) but wanted interview quotes, usability results and before-and-after comparisons that map findings to features. | Partly addressed in the process overview and evaluation results; the poster was not changed. |
| 5 | **Ethics raised by peers** | Musician and location privacy; ticket scalping and fake events; curators' personal taste shaping which local artists are shown, and whether artists and communities can question selections; transparency about curator choices. | Already on our ethics page (curation as gatekeeping, scam events, location). Moderation and a way to question a selection are **not built**. |
| 6 | **City-level only** | One peer (T7) wanted music tied to a more specific location than the city. | Not built. Folio is scoped to a city by design. |

## What peers valued
Human curation that feels personal rather than algorithmic; named local curators and liner notes with sources; no likes, rankings or follower counts; a clear pitch and poster ("Built by people. Shared with the city." and the "A Day in Folio" timeline); the daily "read, then done" idea.

## What this tells us (and what it does not)
It supports two decisions we already made: sourced Liner Notes and no popularity signals. It adds three weaknesses we have not fixed: one-way interaction, unclear demo versus real, and a midnight lock that is hard to demonstrate. It cannot say whether real listeners would use the app after the first week.
