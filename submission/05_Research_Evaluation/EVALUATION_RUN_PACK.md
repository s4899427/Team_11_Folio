## Before each session (10 minutes)
- Live site opens over mobile data (QR code or link). Create no accounts for testers: use *Join as a guest*.
- Print consent slips or read the script below.
- Open the observation sheet (kit) and a timer.

## Consent script (read aloud)
"We're testing an app, not you. You can stop at any time. We'll note what you say and do, using a code instead of your name. If we take a photo, it won't show your face unless you agree. The app can use your city; it won't store your location. Is that okay?" Record "consent given" with the code and the date.

## Run of show (30 to 40 minutes)
1. **Context (3 min).** When and where do you usually find new music? Who do you trust for it?
2. **First screen (2 min).** Hand over the phone with the link open. Say nothing. Ask: "What do you think this is? Would you open it tomorrow? Why or why not?" (This tests the new intro.)
3. **Tasks (15 min), together and unassisted.** T1 to T5 from the kit. Include the groupware moment: two people open the same Edition, one comments, ask the other what they noticed.
4. **Questions (10 min).** Q1 to Q11, prioritising: curator trust, new-versus-familiar balance (Q8), unwanted interaction (Q10), physical and social context (Q11).
5. **The critical question (3 min).** "When would you NOT use this? What would stop you opening it in a day?"
6. **Close (2 min).** Thank them; ask permission to quote anonymously.

## What to write down (one row per finding)
Quote or observation; participant code; which concept or heuristic it touches (awareness, privacy vs visibility, context vs distraction, locales); severity; the change we will make; the commit that makes it. Keep disagreement with our assumptions: it is the strongest evidence of a real process.

## After each session (15 minutes)
Same day: type up notes, assign the code, add findings to the results template, pick the one change to make and tell the builder (Dylan or Pranav).

## Rules that protect the marks
- Do not invent or round up results. Report small samples as small samples.
- Do not coach during tasks. If you have to help, note the failure.
- Keep codes, not names. Photos only with consent.
