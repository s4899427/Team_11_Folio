# Poster and pitch brief (Shaz: poster, design, pitch; Pranav: kit and QR; all pitch)

Start when: the build is frozen, the evaluation round is done, and the ethics notes exist. Current split (from 9 Oct): Shaz owns the poster, design, pitch and demo script; Pranav owns the tradeshow kit and QR code; every member pitches.
Deadline: Week 12 studio (about Tue 20 or Thu 22 Oct, confirm the day). Artefacts are due 3:00 pm Mon 26 Oct 2026.

## What the brief requires (Design Project brief, v1)
- Tradeshow is a **demonstration, not a presentation**. Visitors use the prototype themselves, and it must support **multiple users**.
- **Poster**: A2 (420 x 594 mm), portrait, close-read conference poster covering problem space, concept and process, pulling from the GitHub documentation. Must be self-contained.
- **Pitch**: 2-3 minutes. **Every team member** must be ready to pitch.
- Optional: short video, brochure, hashtag. Link or embed from the repo main page.
- Marked on: a creative, engaging pitch and poster that clearly explain the project (one of five equal criteria).

## Poster content (suggested spine, use evidence only)
1. Problem: doomscrolling and algorithmic feeds, newcomers with no way into a local scene. Cite only sources the team has read.
2. Opportunity and design objective (see docs/evaluation/EVALUATION_KIT.md, section 1).
3. The concept: one human-curated Edition per city per day, curator first, Nearby, Did you know, mix of known and new local artists.
4. Framework: Design for Location (Site, People, Nodes, Trajectory, Public Face).
5. Process: research, requirements, designs, build, evaluation, changes made because of it.
6. Evaluation findings and the decisions they caused (only real findings).
7. Ethics: curator power, voting and gaming, cultural touches, artist-fan connection.
8. Try it: QR code to the live site, how to join as a guest.
Include screenshots and one real quote from the evaluation if a participant agreed.

## Pitch (2-3 minutes) suggested shape
- 0:00 Hook: moving to a new city and knowing none of the music.
- 0:30 What Folio is, in one sentence, and what it is not (no algorithm, no likes).
- 1:00 Hand them the phone: join as guest, open Today, see the curator and the new-and-upcoming pick.
- 1:45 What we learned from testing and what we changed.
- 2:30 Invite them to back a curator and tell us if the Discovery question worked.
Prepare a demo script for two phones, and a backup if Wi-Fi fails (offline demo mode works but is not shared).

## Do not
- Invent statistics, quotes or participants. Label example data as examples.
- Claim Seoul or Mumbai have curators. They do not.
- Put the greetings or Acknowledgement of Country on the poster unless a native speaker or Elder has checked the wording.
