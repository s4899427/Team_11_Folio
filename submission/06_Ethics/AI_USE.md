## How we kept AI use honest
- AI never picked Editions or ranked anything shown to readers.
- Every statistic, quotation and source in documents comes from the team's research or a linked page; nothing was added to look better. Unconfirmed items are marked as unconfirmed.
- Where AI wrote text, a team member read it and is named in the table.
