## Process expectations the brief and slides also state
These are not separate criteria, but a marker will look for them.
- **Domain-owner users, plural, over time.** Interviews: `05_Research_Evaluation/all-interviews-deidentified.md`. Gap: show the later rounds changed the design.
- **More than one idea and multiple prototypes.** The earlier world-culture concept (MOTIF) and the Base44 prototype are in `04_Design_Process/`. Gap: show them as a sequence with the reason for each change.
- **Adapted evaluation methods.** `05_Research_Evaluation/EVALUATION_KIT.md` adapts the Locales heuristics and adds a "when would you NOT use it" question. Gap: run it.
- **Use a design framework.** The brief recommends the location framework. `03_Theory_SMC/SMC_CONCEPT_MAP.md` applies it.
- **Do not let the prototype become the product.** We call the build a proof-of-concept and list what it is not (`02_Product/MVP_SPEC.md`).
