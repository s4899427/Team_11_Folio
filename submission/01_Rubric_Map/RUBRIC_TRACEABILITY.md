## Process expectations the brief and slides also state
These are not separate criteria, but a marker will look for them.
- **Domain-owner users, plural, over time.** Interviews: `05_Research_Evaluation/all-interviews-deidentified.md` (Round 1 and Round 2), then two evaluation sessions on 19 Oct and tradeshow peer feedback. How later rounds changed the design is in the Design Process Overview and `04_Design_Process/pivot-log.md`.
- **More than one idea and multiple prototypes.** The earlier world-culture concept (MOTIF), the Base44 side prototype and the final Folio build are shown as a sequence in `04_Design_Process/`.
- **Adapted evaluation methods.** `05_Research_Evaluation/EVALUATION_KIT.md` adapts the Locales heuristics and adds a "when would you NOT use it" question. It was run in two sessions (`EVALUATION_RESULTS.md`).
- **Use a design framework.** `03_Theory_SMC/SMC_CONCEPT_MAP.md` applies the location framework.
- **Do not let the prototype become the product.** We call the build a proof-of-concept and list what it is not (`02_Product/MVP_SPEC.md`).
